### Week 6 Objectives:

- Understand AWS scalability and high availability concepts.
- Learn how EC2 Auto Scaling automatically adjusts compute capacity.
- Understand Application Load Balancer and health checks.
- Learn the fundamentals of DNS management with Amazon Route 53.
- Understand Amazon CloudFront and Content Delivery Networks.
- Design a highly available AWS application architecture.
